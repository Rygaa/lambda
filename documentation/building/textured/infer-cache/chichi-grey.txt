Paint one flat texture for a top-down 3D pixel art game.
It covers 0.8 x 0.16 meters of "Chichi's house, a cream brick house with a brown tile roof, a white dome house in front, and a wing on the right".
What to paint: Grey stone blocks of a low foundation, in one row.
Main color: #b8b4b0.
The reference image shows the object. Copy the material of this surface from it, but in plain white daylight: never copy the sunset, night, or colored light of the image.

Rules:
- A straight-on flat view of the surface that fills the whole image, edge to edge. No perspective, no background, no border, no frame, no text.
- The image will be stretched to a 0.8:0.16 area, so place details for that shape.
- Simple pixel art: big flat color areas, clear shapes, few colors.
- Most of the image is the main color. Joints, lines, and marks are one or two steps lighter or darker in the same hue.
- A material repeats, so its opposite edges must meet with no seams, and it has no signs or one-time marks.
- No painted light, no shadows, no outlines, no gradients. The game adds light and outlines.
- The final texture is only 35 x 7 pixels, so only big details survive.
