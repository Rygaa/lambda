hair.glb: the hair of HairSample_Male.vrm, a sample model of the VRoid project (pixiv), its 114 strands only (one mesh
each), fitted on the FlyFF head at rest (scaled, turned to face +z), with its texture. The gold form of the
transformation bends it into a Super Saiyan hair (web/lib/superHair.js).
https://opengameart.org/content/vroid-studio-cc0-models (hairsample_male.zip)
